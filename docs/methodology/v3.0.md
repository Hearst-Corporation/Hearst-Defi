# Hearst Mining Note — Methodology v3.0

**Status**: Active
**Effective from**: 2026-07-15
**Owner**: Product + Risk
**Supersedes (product model)**: v1.0 (rule-based yield projection) and v2.0 (Monte Carlo
extension) remain **immutable on-file references** for any memo generated under them; v3.0 is
the **active methodology** for the current product — the **mining note** on
`PermissionedDynaVault v2.1`. The unratified `v2.1-draft` (multi-vault + share classes for the
yield vault) is **not** built upon here.
**ADR**: ADR-019 (product-model replacement) — see also ADR-006 (guardrails), ADR-003 (mining
sourcing), ADR-010 (superseded contract).

## 0. Why v3.0 and not v2.1 / a patch

The product changed from a **monthly-distribution, blended-APY yield vault** to a **BTC-
accumulation mining note**. Per the versioning convention carried in this series, a change that
makes projections **non-comparable** with the previous version is a **MAJOR bump**. Replacing a
distributed-yield model with a BTC-accumulation model is exactly that, so this is `v3.0`, not a
minor revision. v1.0 and v2.0 stay immutable and on-file (audit trail): a memo generated under
an earlier version keeps its original `methodology_version` and is never retroactively rewritten.

## 1. Scope

This document defines, for the mining note:

- what the product **is** (3 pockets, 24-month term, BTC accumulation);
- how its **estimated return** is projected and **disclosed** (as a range, in accumulated BTC);
- the **mechanisms** that shape outcomes (take-profit, vending curve, curtailment, electricity);
- the **provenance, PTAI, forbidden-vocabulary and disclaimer** discipline that every output
  must satisfy.

It is **immutable once published**. Any change requires a new version (`v3.1` / `v4.0`) and an ADR.

## 2. Product model

The mining note is a **BTC-accumulation instrument backed by real Bitcoin mining**, structured
in **3 on-chain pockets** whose allocation is **fixed on-chain**:

| Pocket | Name | Allocation | On-chain (bps) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** | Mining Power | 40% | 4000 |
| **B2** | BTC Pouch | 27% | 2700 |
| **B3** | Reserve USDC | 33% | 3300 |

- **Term**: 24 months (`productDurationMonths()` / `currentMonth()`).
- **Return delivery**: BTC is **accumulated over the term and delivered at maturity**. There is
  **no periodic cash distribution** and **no fixed APY**.
- **Applicative vs on-chain**: the $250k **minimum ticket** and the 60-day **soft lock-up** are
  **contractual / applicative** — the contract does **not** enforce a `minDeposit` or a lock-up.
  The on-chain gates are `tvlCap` (capacity) and `whitelist` (permissioned access).
- **Asset**: USDC (6 decimals).

## 3. Estimated return (projection & disclosure)

The note does not pay a rate. Its **estimated return is projected as a range, expressed in
accumulated BTC**, and always shown with its assumptions and a stressed view.

- **Headline is always a range** (non-negotiable #1): e.g. `8–15%`. **No single-point figure is
  ever published.** The range mechanic is preserved from earlier methodologies; only its
  **meaning** changes — it now describes **accumulated BTC, not distributed and not guaranteed**.
- **Provenance**: `Estimated` (model / derived), with the methodology link and freshness surfaced.
- **Inputs** feeding the projection: mining economics (see §4), the take-profit tiers, the
  vending schedule, and the curtailment thresholds — each is a **configured parameter or an
  observed input**, never a promised performance number.

## 4. Mining economics (input to accumulation)

```
mining_revenue      = deployed_hashrate (TH/s) × hashprice ($/TH/day) × uptime (%)
operating_costs     = energy_cost + hosting_cost + pool_fee + maintenance
mining_gross_margin = mining_revenue − operating_costs
```

Hearst does not operate ASICs directly; the note buys exposure via **revenue-share** with 1–2
partner farms (ADR-003). Net mining margin **accrues to the note as BTC accumulation** (pockets
B1/B2). The mining pocket's electricity is settled on-chain through the **electricity account**
(`payElectricity()` / `elecStatus()` / `monthlyElecCost()`). On-chain mining is read via
`miningMetrics()` → (`hashrateTh`, `totalBtcEarnedSats`), `Attested` provenance.

## 5. Mechanisms

Allocation is fixed (40/27/33); outcomes are shaped by three on-chain mechanisms, not by sleeve
reallocation. All parameters below are **configured contract values**, disclosed as such.

### 5.1 Take-profit (B2 BTC Pouch)
BTC profit is realised at **configured price tiers**: `setTakeProfitTier` /
`executeTakeProfit`, emitting `TakeProfitExecuted`. Take-profit is a **realisation event**, not
a distribution to LPs.

### 5.2 Vending curve (B3 Reserve USDC)
B3 depletes along a **fixed schedule** across the term to fund operations:
`vendingCurveBps(month)` over `productDurationMonths()`. The curve is a parameter of the note,
known at inception.

### 5.3 Curtailment (B1 Mining Power)
When BTC price is below the **configured pre/post-halving threshold**, the mining pocket is
**curtailed** (`isCurtailed()` / `curtail()` / `liftCurtailment()`; thresholds via
`setCurtailmentThresholds` + `setHalvingMonth`, emitting `CurtailmentTriggered` /
`CurtailmentLifted`). Curtailment protects margin; it is not a performance claim.

### 5.4 Keeper / monthly engine
The privileged monthly operations (`rebalance()`, `payElectricity()`, `reportMiningMetrics()`)
run through a deterministic **keeper**, kill-switched (`KEEPER_ENABLED`, default **OFF**) with a
server-only key, human-gated, and **never** exposed to the chat or any agent/crew (ADR-012 /
ADR-017 / ADR-018). See ADR-019 for the keeper-signing arbitration.

## 6. Confidence & assumptions

Every projection **shows its assumptions** (non-negotiable #10) and degrades confidence with
stale or out-of-range inputs (data freshness, historical fit, out-of-range assumptions,
operational headroom). A low-confidence projection **must** state that fact in its narrative.

## 7. Provenance ladder

Every metric carries one badge, in descending order of data quality:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Live** | Real-time or < 5 min old, direct from primary source |
| **Oracle** | On-chain / signed-data oracle, may be up to 1h old |
| **Attested** | Partner-signed attestation (e.g. mining uptime, hashrate, energy) |
| **Estimated** | Derived / modelled (e.g. projected return range) |
| **Manual** | Human-entered, no automated source |
| **Stale** | Any source past its freshness SLA |

A metric inherits the **weakest** badge of its inputs; any `Stale` input forces `Stale`.
Downgrading is automatic; upgrading requires a fresh fetch.

## 8. PTAI format

Every simulation and rebalancing recommendation follows **PTAI**:

```
Projection  — What the model expects (range, not point estimate)
Trigger     — The condition that would warrant action
Action      — The specific pocket / curtailment / take-profit step recommended
Impact      — Quantified effect on BTC-accumulation range, term, provenance
```

Auto-execution of privileged actions is **human-gated**. The Action field never contains a
commitment to future returns; it uses ranges and conditional language.

## 9. Forbidden vocabulary

Forbidden in all agent outputs, memos, and investor-facing text: `guarantee` / `guaranteed`,
`promise`, `certain`, `will deliver` / `will generate` / `will produce` (forward-looking),
`risk-free` / `riskless` / `no risk` / `zero risk`. The phrase **"not guaranteed"** is
explicitly **permitted and encouraged** in disclaimers and assumption blocks.

## 10. Disclaimers (appended verbatim to every projection)

> Projections are conditional on stated assumptions. Past performance does not guarantee future
> results. This product is a mining note that accumulates Bitcoin over a 24-month term and
> delivers BTC at maturity — it makes no periodic cash distribution and carries no fixed APY.
> Estimated returns are expressed as a range in accumulated BTC and are not guaranteed. Offered
> exclusively to professional / qualified investors via a Cayman Exempted Limited Partnership.
> Minimum subscription and soft lock-up are contractual / applicative and not enforced on-chain.
> Not an offer or solicitation where prohibited.

## 11. Versioning

- Bump on any material change to the model or disclosure; a bump always requires a new ADR.
- Every memo PDF references the `methodology_version` at generation time.
- Old versions remain on file for audit; a LP may request the version used for their entry.
