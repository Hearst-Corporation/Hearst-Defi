# Hearst Connect — Vision

## What it is

A single institutional product — a **mining note** running on **PermissionedDynaVault v2.1** —
that **accumulates Bitcoin**, backed by real BTC mining, structured in **3 on-chain pockets**:

1. **B1 · Mining Power (40%)** — exposure to real Bitcoin mining cashflow via revenue-share
   with partner farm(s) (ADR-003). Net mining margin accrues to the note in BTC; the pocket's
   electricity is settled on-chain through the electricity account (`payElectricity()`), never
   as a cash payout to LPs.
2. **B2 · BTC Pouch (27%)** — BTC accumulated and held, sized by rules; profit-taking at
   configured price tiers (`setTakeProfitTier` / `executeTakeProfit`).
3. **B3 · Reserve USDC (33%)** — USDC reserve that funds operations over the term, depleting
   along a fixed **vending curve** across the 24-month term (`vendingCurveBps`).

Allocation is **fixed on-chain** at 40 / 27 / 33 (bps 4000 / 2700 / 3300) — it is not a
variable sleeve mix.

## Headline

The note **accumulates BTC over a 24-month term** and **delivers BTC at maturity**. There is
**no periodic cash distribution** and **no fixed APY**.

- **Estimated target return** — mining note, expressed in **accumulated BTC, not distributed,
  not guaranteed**: **8–15%** range, always shown with its assumptions and a stressed view.
  Provenance: `Estimated`. Never a single point — always a range.
- **Term**: 24 months (`productDurationMonths()`).
- **Minimum ticket**: $250k — **contractual / applicative** (v2 has no on-chain `minDeposit`).
- **Soft lock-up**: 60 days — **contractual, not enforced on-chain** (the on-chain gates are
  `tvlCap` + `whitelist`; there is no on-chain lock-up).
- **Fees**: 1% mgmt + 10% perf (high-watermark), applicative.
- **Structure**: Cayman Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP), professional investors only.

## Three product promises

1. **Lisibility** — a LP must understand the strategy in 5 minutes.
2. **Simulability** — every assumption must be stressable interactively.
3. **Auditability** — proof of reserves, on-chain contract events, published methodology.

## What we are NOT

- Not a monthly-distribution / fixed-APY yield product — the note accumulates BTC and delivers
  it at maturity.
- Not a quant terminal (no VaR/CVaR/Sharpe headline).
- Not retail.
- Not a DeFi protocol — we are a **manager** consuming protocols.
